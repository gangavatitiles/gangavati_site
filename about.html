About

A showroom for surfaces chosen with care.

Gangavati Tiles helps you look at tile in real light, for homes and commercial spaces, before a decision is made.

What we can say with confidence is modest, and that is intentional. A founding date, a client count, and a list of awards have not been supplied, so they are not published here.

The showroom is at Shop No.6, New Poonam Regency CHS, on MTNL Road near DCB Bank, Sai Baba Nagar, Mira Road East. Visitors come to compare how a surface looks underfoot and on the wall, and to ask what is currently available.

This website introduces that visit. Photographs are licensed references for atmosphere and material, not a record of Gangavati installations.

Design philosophy

Material before mythology.

A floor or wall is chosen for touch, light, and the way a room is used. Marble-look stone, honed surfaces, wood effects, and patterns each ask for a different kind of attention. The useful conversation happens in front of the material.

Showroom experience

See it before you specify it.

Bring the room you are planning. We can look at colour, finish, and where a surface might belong. Sizes, slip ratings, and stock are confirmed from current showroom information, not from placeholder copy on this site.
