The showroom

Thoughtfully Selected. Beautifully Crafted.

Gangavati Tiles is a showroom for people choosing surfaces for homes and commercial spaces. The aim is simple: help you compare materials in person, in real light, before you decide.

Marble looks, stone, wood effects, and patterned surfaces each change a room in a different way. The collection on this site is a starting point. Availability and specifications are confirmed at the showroom.
